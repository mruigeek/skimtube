# Privacy Policy for SkimTube

**Last Updated: July 31, 2026**

SkimTube ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is designed to provide high-quality video summaries and digests locally and privately. This Privacy Policy details our data governance practices, commitment to user privacy, and compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Usage

### A. Personal Data
SkimTube **does not** collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location data.

### B. Device Storage & Local Preferences
The Application stores minimal user settings locally on your device via standard encryption-backed `SharedPreferences`. This includes:
- Monitored channel lists and custom RSS configurations.
- Saved bookmark references.
- User-specified API server endpoint preferences.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any external analytics platform or third-party tracking server.

---

## 2. Network Communications & Transcripts

SkimTube communicates exclusively with:
1. **Configured API Backend**: To request and render video digests and RSS sync updates.
2. **Public Video Metadata & Transcripts**: Fetched directly or via backend service for natural language processing and summary generation.

No user usage telemetry, analytics trackers, or advertising IDs (GAID) are integrated into SkimTube.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all preferences and saved bookmarks are stored locally on your device:
- You can clear all application data at any time via Android Application Settings -> **Clear Data / Clear Storage**.
- Uninstalling SkimTube completely removes all locally stored preferences and data.
